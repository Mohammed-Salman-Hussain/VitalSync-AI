1. Problem
Top 1-3 urgent problems faced by users
  • The Cyberchondria Panic Loop: Commercial search engines rank rare, catastrophic pathologies (ALS, heart failure) for common benign sensations (eyelid twitches, post-coffee palpitations, 1.5kg scale jumps), driving severe health anxiety.
  • Fragmented & Siloed Tools: Users calculate calories on one site, macros on another, and sleep debt elsewhere; no tools communicate or cross-factor bedtime screens and caffeine half-life.
  • Cloud Privacy & Telemetry Threat: Fitness trackers upload intimate biometrics to cloud brokers and ad networks.
  • Hallucinating Cloud LLMs: Generic chatbots guess unverified caloric numbers and medical claims without clinical guardrails.

WebMD / Healthline (alarmist SEO ads), generic ChatGPT/Claude (hallucinating black boxes), MyFitnessPal (isolated calorie counter), Whoop/Oura ($300+/yr cloud paywalls).
