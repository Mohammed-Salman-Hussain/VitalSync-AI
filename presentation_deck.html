  • Millions search common bodily signals: eyelid twitches, post-coffee heart palpitations, or 1.5kg scale jumps.

  • Average worker spends 7-10 hours on screens plus 45-90 minutes doomscrolling in bed.

  • Causes 30-60 min sleep latency, chronic sleep debt, and metabolic decline.
